## Using Syncify

- **Age:** you need to be at least 13, and old enough where you live to agree to these terms.

- **Limits:** Syncify limits how often and how much you can back up, share and post, so it can stay free.

## What you share

- **It stays yours:** share codes, Discover posts and their images, your handle and your socials are yours. You let Syncify store them, show them on its website and in the extension, and copy or resize them as it needs to run, until you delete them. The privacy note says how long copies stay after that.
- **Public by design:** anyone with a code or its link can see what's in it and apply it in their own Spotify, and your profile page lists all your codes. Public Discover posts, handles and profile pages can be seen by anyone.
- **Discover posts:** follow [what's allowed on Discover](https://syncify.wsoltani.com/discover/rules). Images are checked automatically, and some by a person, before they go public.
- **Handles:** a handle can't pretend to be someone else or be offensive. Syncify can reset one that does, which also removes your socials.

## Supporting Syncify

- **A gift:** support on Ko-fi is a gift to a free project. Everything Syncify does for everyone stays free, and supporting doesn't buy more of it.
- **Thanks:** supporters get extras like a badge, a profile color and pinned backups. They're thanks, not a paid service, and they can change.
- **Payments:** Ko-fi takes the payment under its own terms. Syncify never sees or holds your money details. Refunds and monthly memberships are handled on Ko-fi.

## Not allowed

- Getting into backups, accounts or installs that aren't yours
- Getting around Syncify's limits, or flooding it with requests

- Using reports to harass someone
- Anything illegal where you live

If you break these terms, Syncify can delete your codes or posts, reset your handle, stop you from posting to Discover, ban your account for a while or for good, or delete it. A ban signs you out, takes your codes, posts and profile page off the website, and locks your secured backups until it ends. Deleting an account also deletes the backups of every Spotify username linked to it. If you think Syncify got it wrong, say so in the Discord or at [syncify@wsoltani.com](mailto:syncify@wsoltani.com).
